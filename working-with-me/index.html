Working with me

The practical details.

What you'd ask on a first call, answered in writing.

Availability

Available now for permanent roles, contracts, a few days on a project or ad hoc tickets. Remote is ideal; hybrid or on-site in the UK for the right role. Based in the UK, abroad for now; back within days for an agreed UK role. For longer contracts, full time or 3–4 days a week works; around six months is a preference, not a minimum.

Hours

I set my day to your team’s. From abroad: UK afternoons (from 2pm UK, 3pm in summer) or the US working day, Pacific included. Back in the UK: full UK hours.

Right to work

UK citizen. No visa or sponsorship needed for UK roles.

Contracts and permanent roles

  • Tell me your salary range, contract rate or project budget; I'll tell you straight away if it fits.
  • UK-registered sole trader, invoicing directly. Outside the UK: Deel, Remote or Wise.
  • For a longer engagement, we can start with a two-week paid trial on a defined piece of work. For a small job, we agree the scope and price before starting.
  • Agencies: I can set up a limited company for outside-IR35 work.
  • Permanent roles: tell me the package; the paperwork is a Deel or Remote contract, or direct once I'm back. No sponsorship needed.

Communication

Mostly written. A short update at the end of each day: what shipped, what's next, what I need from you. Decisions live in the pull request or issue. Happy to join a stand-up in shared hours.

What I actually do

I work efficiently with Claude Code. Most of the code on SpiralClass is agent-written; I review it, test it and run it in production, with automated checks before changes are merged. My earlier work was in PHP, Magento and Laravel; SpiralClass runs on TypeScript, Next.js and Postgres. I can work through requirements with clients, connect payments and other services, and take the work through to running it. See the case study and source and the engineering notes.

How I work

  • Start with users. I talk to them, read what they actually say, and change the plan when the evidence points somewhere else.
  • Experiments have a stopping rule. The hypothesis, what to measure and when to kill it are written down before any code.
  • I own what the agents write. I build with coding agents, and every change goes through a pull request, tests and a check in a real browser.

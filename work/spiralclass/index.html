Payments that skip the platform

The first design collected money on the platform and passed it on. That broke payouts in the launch market and put the platform in the middle of disputes.

Now Stripe charges on the teacher's own account and pays her locally. Wise covers teachers outside Stripe.

One catch: a live checkout showed cards only, though the account said local methods were on. It had inherited a dashboard setting. I fixed it and checked OXXO and SPEI in a real checkout. A green flag isn't proof. The longer version is in my notes.

The teacher owns the method

One teacher had her own lesson structure; the AI lesson writer ignored it. Now teachers save templates, and the AI fills the chosen one for each student's level and goals. Optional, and it works for any subject.

Captions and learning notes

The teacher turns on live captions and each side sees the other's words as they are spoken, then the translation when the phrase ends. The browser does the recognising and, where it can, the translating. Deepgram covers a phone; Google covers the translation when the browser can't. No server listens, so it costs nothing to run. D-185 says why the earlier server-side design went.

With the student's consent the same text is kept as the class transcript. After class, Claude turns it into findings in five areas, each tied to the line that showed it. The teacher confirms or dismisses each one before it counts. The confirmed ones build the student's profile and a short brief before the next class.

Rules where they matter

  • A cancelled class restores or forfeits the right credit. classify.ts
  • Each payment reconciles to the right student and package. reconcile-paid.ts, wise-reconcile.ts
  • Students see only their own teacher's content. SECURITY.md says a missing teacher scope is a vulnerability, not a style nit.

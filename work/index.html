WantToJoin

Tonight's outings at a hostel, and who's going, on one link.

Why
I read 2,800 messages from a hostel's WhatsApp group first. Hosts posted the same outing all day, so I built for hosts, not guests.
Hard part
Fitting how hosts already work. AI drafts each post in English and Spanish; code checks the times, place and price.
Now
One-week pilot at that hostel. Results, good or bad, go on the case study.

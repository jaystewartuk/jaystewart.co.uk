WantToJoin

wanttojoin.com · live pilot at one hostel · case study

A page per hostel that turns the evening's WhatsApp chaos into one link. Before writing code I read 2,800 messages from a hostel's guest WhatsApp group. The finding set the direction: its hosts are the customer, not the guests.

  • From research to a pilot in three days, built with the hosts from what they actually did.
  • An AI post writer that drafts every post in English and Spanish from the form's facts, checked in code. All 50 eval drafts pass, at about $0.0003 a post.
  • Results at the end of the one-week pilot, on 14 October.

Beckon

source · staging verified · not in production

A remote inbox for terminal agents and long-running jobs. When a process is blocked, Beckon can notify you; paired devices can respond without sending keystrokes through the server. I turned a pattern in developer discussions into a product hypothesis, then built the CLI, service and installable web app end to end.

  • A Go helper wraps commands in a PTY and reads OSC 7501 status, with prompt detection for tools that do not report status themselves.
  • Messages are end-to-end encrypted. Pairing compares a six-word safety code, and the helper checks signed responses against locally trusted devices before typing an offered choice.
  • Web Push, live session updates, a mobile PWA, account plans and billing are implemented; production deployment and external user validation remain ahead.

SpiralClass

spiralclass.com · demo · source

A scheduling, payments and video platform for independent teachers. A teacher publishes a booking page and sells packages of classes; her students book, join the lesson in the browser and get materials afterwards; she gets paid. It runs in production with nine real users and one maintainer, so it is sized for correctness rather than scale.

  • TypeScript end to end: Next.js 15, Postgres through Prisma, 82 models, durable background jobs.
  • Two payment rails: Stripe Connect direct charges on the teacher's own account, and bank transfers through Wise, reconciled against its API.
  • One definition of green, run the same way locally and in CI, including an integration suite against a real database with a migration drift check.
  • More than a hundred decision records, several of them reversing an earlier call and saying why.
